# THUNDERDOME — Content Roadmap

The 10-rank ladder clears too fast once a kitted crew comes online. The goal is to
stretch a career into a long arc without diluting the core loop (lethal bouts →
blood money → gear/crew decisions → harder bouts).

## Phase 1 — Deeper ladder (mostly data + AI variants; fastest payoff)

- **15 ranks.** Five new squads slotted into the curve, steeper gear/medical prices
  in the last third so a good crew stays expensive to maintain.
- **Enemy archetypes** (bolt onto existing Combatant with flags, like `boss`):
  - *Medic* — bandages squadmates mid-fight; priority-target gameplay.
  - *Shield* — frontal riot shield (torso immune from the front); flank or frag.
  - *Rusher* — shotgun sprinter, extra HP, ignores caution; punishes campers.
  - *Marksman* — DMR with a visible laser telegraph; controls lanes.
- **Circuits (NG+).** Beating Rank 1 rolls the career into Circuit 2: same ladder,
  global modifiers (enemy HP/accuracy up, payouts up, remixed squad rosters and
  names). Infinite scaling = the long game; champion belt count on the menu.
- **Pre-match betting.** Bet on yourself with odds scaled by rank and your record.
- **New tournament-master events:** BOUNTY (marked enemy pays 3×), WEAPON RAIN
  (random guns clatter into the pit), CHAMPION'S WHIM (20s pistols-only).

## Phase 2 — Venues + gear depth

- **Two new arenas**, rotating by rank band:
  - *The Docks* — container maze, crane catwalk spine, water hazard edge.
  - *The Foundry* — molten channels (area denial), tight catwalk verticality.
  Arena system is already data-driven (colliders + navmesh build automatically);
  each venue is a build function + spawns + screen placement.
- **Weapon mods**, 2 slots per gun: extended mag, compensator (recoil), laser
  (hipfire spread), suppressor (AI reacts slower to your shots). Buy per-copy so
  crew guns can be modded too.
- **Two new guns:** LMG (suppression niche, slow handling), auto-shotgun (late-game
  money sink).
- **Crew XP.** Mercs level from career kills: small stat bumps per level, visible
  on the roster card. Makes the long-lived crew an emotional investment.

## Phase 3 — Characters + spectacle

- **Named bosses** at ranks 5 / 3 / 1 with gimmicks; GOLIATH gets phases (mask
  shatters at 50% → headshots open up).
- **Recurring rival squad** that survives across circuits, taunts you through
  VULTURE, and comes back tougher each meeting.
- **Sponsor contracts:** optional bout objectives ("win without medkits",
  "3 headshots") for bonus purses.
- **Crowd escalation:** frenzy states where the crowd throws weapons/molotovs,
  chants tied to your streaks.
